## Sound effects and materials

Engine harmonics, wind noise and pickup effects are original Web Audio synthesis, not recordings of a real aircraft. Terrain/wood textures are generated deterministically in code. No external texture dependencies.

## Reference artwork

01-grona-dalen.webp: user-selected Golden Valley Flight Challenge reference.
02-bergsaventyret.webp: new generated alpine valley reference.
03-kustflygningen.webp: new generated Swedish coast reference.
04-solnedgangen.webp: new generated sunset countryside reference.
05-molnaventyret.webp: new generated high-cloud valley reference.

References 02–05 were created individually with the built-in image generator in 16:9. Shared prompt: photorealistic premium 3D flight game art direction, chase camera behind a white/red propeller aircraft, fixed gear, gold coin route, turbines, distant runway, physically believable materials, soft light, no UI. Individual prompts specify alpine morning; Swedish coast midday; copper sunset countryside; high cloud valley respectively. These are visual targets, not screenshots or scenery backgrounds. The game renders geometry independently.
