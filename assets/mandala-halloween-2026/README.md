# Egna färgläggningsbilder: Halloween

27 originalmotiv skapade med det inbyggda bildgenereringsverktyget. Varje motiv har en helsidesförhandsbild och en separat A4-PDF.

Stil: svart konturteckning på vit bakgrund, tydliga slutna färgläggningsytor, inga färger, skuggor, texter eller vattenmärken. Stående A4 med utskriftsmarginaler. Halloweenmotiven är lekfulla och barnvänliga.

## Motiv och bildprompter

1. **Pumpalyktan** — one large cheerful carved jack-o-lantern with curling vines, leaves and tiny stars
2. **Pumpafamiljen** — five different friendly carved jack-o-lanterns arranged with patterned leaves
3. **Det glada spöket** — a friendly floating ghost holding a little pumpkin bucket among stars and swirls
4. **Det tokiga spökhuset** — a whimsical haunted house with crooked towers, pumpkins, friendly ghosts and a moon
5. **Häxan på kvasten** — a friendly witch in a pointed hat flying on a broom across a moonlit sky
6. **Häxans hatt** — a large pointed witch hat with decorative stars, a buckle and curling ribbons
7. **Den magiska kitteln** — a whimsical cauldron bubbling with outlined bubbles and stars, with pumpkins nearby
8. **Halloweenkatten** — a friendly cat wearing a tiny witch hat sitting next to a carved pumpkin, outline fur only
9. **Fladdermusen** — a large cute bat with patterned wings flying in front of a crescent moon
10. **Spindelns nät** — a friendly spider sitting in an intricate but clearly spaced web with leaves and stars
11. **Det dansande skelettet** — a friendly cartoon skeleton dancing with a bow tie among pumpkins and musical notes
12. **Den busiga mumien** — a friendly cartoon mummy with clear bandage strips waving beside a pumpkin
13. **Det snälla monstret** — a friendly stitched cartoon monster with bolts and a bow tie surrounded by stars
14. **Den vänliga vampyren** — a cheerful cartoon vampire in a cape with a pumpkin bucket and small bats

16. **Monsterkalas** — three friendly funny monsters with different horns, eyes and patterned outfits at a party

21. **Halloweenugglan** — a friendly owl wearing a witch hat perched on a crooked branch beside a crescent moon
22. **Trolldrycksflaskorna** — five whimsical magic potion bottles with decorative stoppers, bubbles and star labels without text

26. **Halloweenkrans** — a circular wreath of small pumpkins, friendly bats, ghosts, leaves and stars
27. **Fladdermusslottet** — a whimsical spooky castle on a hill with flying bats, a full moon and cheerful pumpkins
