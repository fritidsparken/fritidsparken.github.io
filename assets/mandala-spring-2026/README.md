20 nya motiv genererade med det inbyggda bildgenereringsverktyget den 18 oktober 2026. Varje motiv har en WebP-förhandsvisning och en separat A4-PDF. De tidigare säsongsmallarna har ersatts enligt beställningen.

## Gemensam bildprompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original printable spring coloring page for Swedish primary school children aged 6–10. Create ONE polished portrait A4 coloring-sheet illustration. SUBJECT: {subject}. Style: elegant hand-drawn black ink outline art, uniform clean medium-bold strokes, pure white paper background, absolutely no gray shading, no filled black areas, no color, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Most shapes must form closed clear spaces for children to color. Attractive balanced composition centered with generous 8% blank print-safe margins. Medium detail, recognizable natural motifs, friendly but not babyish. Fill roughly 80% of the page with the illustration. Crisp high-resolution print-quality line work, no panels, no mockup, no page curl.
