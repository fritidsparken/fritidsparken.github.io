# Origami research register

Research date: 2026-11-15. This register covers all 48 projects in the requested brief. It is a research inventory, **not a claim that 48 animations have been implemented, tested, or physically folded**.

## Evidence levels and release rule

- **S — source description checked:** the public step sequence was read. This establishes what the source teaches, not that the animation implements it correctly.
- **V — source diagrams inspected:** specified diagrams were also visually inspected. Exact geometry still needs an independent implementation and review.
- **D — discovered:** a model/source link or catalogue entry was found, but the complete sequence has not been reviewed. Do not ship from this evidence alone.
- **P — pending:** a variant or important material detail is not yet selected/verified.

No physical folding tests have been performed. No source photographs, diagrams, PDFs, or videos are licensed for redistribution by this research. Temporary reference images are outside the repository and must not enter the deployed assets. Public activities must have their own complete, continuous geometry; source links must not be used to disguise incomplete local instructions.

## First implementation candidates: concise independent sequence notes

These paraphrases are engineering notes; source diagrams/video must still be checked against the implemented geometry. "Straight hinge" does not mean every paper layer can move together indiscriminately.

### Kattansikte — simple variant

Source: https://www.origami-instructions.com/origami-cat-face.html

Use a square, light side toward the viewer. Fold one diagonal into a triangle with apex upward. Crease the triangle's vertical symmetry axis by half-folding, then unfold that half-fold. Raise the right side corner upward at an angle to make an ear; mirror on the left. Fold the remaining central upper tip downward. Turn the complete model over. Face marks are optional drawing. Keep the cat's forehead fold distinct from the tulip below: a cat preview must not depict an unrelated shape.

### Hundansikte — first variant

Source: https://www.origami-instructions.com/origami-dog-face.html

Start light side up and fold the square diagonally so the triangle points downward. Half-fold and unfold the triangle to locate the center. Lower each side corner to make a hanging ear. Fold the small forehead and chin tips away from the viewer. Optional drawn eyes/nose/mouth finish the face. This is the first source variant, not its later eight-step alternative.

### Tulpanblomma — flat flower head only

Source: https://origamiok.com/how-to-make-origami-flat-flower/

Fold a light-side-up square into an upward-pointing triangle. Half-fold the triangle and unfold to mark its vertical center. Raise one side point upward and a little outward; mirror on the other side so the center apex remains visible. Turn over, fold the two small side tips inward, then turn over again. This completes the blossom. Source's full plant also folds a separate stem and cuts the blossom base before insertion; omit both stem and cut only when presenting a blossom, not a complete plant.

### Hjärta — simple diamond-based variant

Source: https://www.origami-instructions.com/easy-origami-heart.html

Orient the square as a diamond, light side up. Fold/unfold along both diagonals. Bring the upper point to the center. Bring the lower point to the upper straight edge. Fold the lower-right slanted section up until its edge follows the vertical center; mirror on the left. Turn over. Fold tiny outer points inward and the two upper points down to soften the shoulders. Turn over again. Source retrieval has a redirect loop on direct opens but its complete indexed instruction text was retrieved with targeted search on 2026-11-15.

### Julgran — diagram checks

Source: https://origami.me/christmas-tree/

Use a diamond oriented square with a vertical center crease. Bring both upper edges to center, creating a kite. Bring the lower tip to the upper tip. Bring the new upper edges to center again. Fold the long top point downward through the source's paired registration points; this reveals the colored triangular canopy behind it. Fold the resulting lowest white tip upward so its point reaches the lower edge of the colored canopy. Turn over for the tree and white planter/trunk. The diagram's step-6 target points, not an arbitrary fractional-height fold, determine this hinge. Diagrams 3–8 were inspected visually; reference-only PNGs were kept outside the repository.

### Klassisk pil and a legitimate broad-wing alternative

Sources: https://www.foldnfly.com/1.html and https://www.foldnfly.com/0.html

Both use a vertical A4 center crease, opened again. Fold the two upper corners to that crease. For the dart, narrow the nose again by bringing the two new sloping edges to center. Fold the model in half along its body and fold each wing independently to the body's lower edge. Spread the wings into flight position. The broad-wing Basic omits the second narrowing pair. These are distinct source sequences, but any local performance descriptions must remain qualitative until actual flight tests exist.

## Relevant attribution and source-use finding

Foldable Flight's [published terms](https://www.foldableflight.com/terms-of-service-and-refund-policy) state that its designs/instructions and media should not be publicly redistributed without written permission. This research did not obtain permission. The user's brief permits model substitutions: use independently animated traditional/classic planes or obtain the appropriate permission before shipping the named proprietary tutorials. A source link alone is not permission. Do not copy source speed/glide/accuracy numbers into claims about an independently modified model.

Origami.me's general catalogue is useful for attribution discovery, but many contemporary named models have individual designers. A catalogue label is not a completed geometry validation or a media license. Images and text must be independently authored; retain appropriate source/designer credit for the selected variant.

## Remaining work before claiming the original brief complete

1. Choose and verify each still-discovered/pending variant, including every material count and any cuts/glue.
2. Build selected-layer pocket, squash, reverse, swivel, petal, crimp, inflation and multi-sheet assembly operations where required. A generic folding triangle cannot represent those operations.
3. Compare every rendered intermediate state and layer identity with the selected source. Preserve sheet area, connectivity, sides, fold reversibility and order.
4. Test complete navigation and every source-specific sequence; test final mechanism for planes, frog, spinner and flapping bird. Physical tests have not occurred in this research.
5. Publish only complete validated activities, and report the actual count without counting this research register as completed activities.
