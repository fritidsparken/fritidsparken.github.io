# Eight new animal faces: source inspection and geometry derivation

Recovered from the retained authoring and QA session after the environment reset. The model module reconstructs the final frozen geometry, including the corrected panda rear fold and short bear ears. The checks and visual inspections below describe that original session; no new rendering was performed during recovery.

All eight complete diagram sheets below were downloaded as research references and visually inspected in the original task. No source images are included in the authored model module or intended site assets. Swedish wording, coordinates, face marks and animations are newly authored. Seven diagrams identify Fumiaki Shingu as copyright holder; the fox diagram labels the model traditional and credits its diagram to Fumiaki Shingu. Model metadata preserves those distinctions. Public diagram availability is not a claim that source artwork may be republished.

The source diagrams are schematic, so fold positions not set by exact corner alignment were measured as proportions and authored in millimetres. Every model begins with one real 150 × 150 mm sheet, drawn as a diamond with half-diagonal R = 150 / sqrt(2). Each fold partitions existing paper and rotates complete rigid panels; no outline is replaced and no paper is discarded. Simultaneous left/right operations in sources are expanded into individual animated steps. Finishing marks explicitly mean drawing with a pen.

## Derivations

- **Fox:** Diagram sequence forms an upward triangle, lowers its apex to form a trapezoid, then lifts both side wings to make long ears and turns the model over. The apex fold is the exact perpendicular bisector y = −0.5R. Ear axes run from the lower centre to (±0.52R, −0.5R), making a narrow separation between the ear tips. The remaining downward face is pointed. This differs from the existing cat's broad forehead and its short side ears. Source sheet has out-of-order printed step numbers; geometry follows the arrows and shapes.
- **Rabbit:** Diagram 1–8. A narrow 0.16R strip is raised along the triangle's base, then the side corners meet the centre on 45-degree axes. That strip supplies the long ears. The forehead and chin are folded behind after turning over. Both entire ear lineages are excluded from the forehead fold, preserving their complete tips. Pen marks are placed on the broad central face.
- **Panda:** Diagram 1–7. Two centre creases establish the axes. Side corners are brought to ±0.2R, leaving broader side columns than the pig. The upper half folds backward and down, narrow outer margins fold behind, and the lower tip makes the muzzle. Only the front white layer of the raised muzzle folds back behind the dark triangle. Start with the light side facing up and take the upper half behind the lower half; this preserves the visible dark folded corner markings. Oval pen patches distinguish the eyes; all paper remains present.
- **Pig:** Diagram 1–7. Side corners meet the centre exactly, then the top folds down. The bottom fold raises the snout; its front tip is folded down on a separate crease, yielding the little diamond. Upper corners fold diagonally inward into short ears. Its folded head proportions, ear treatment and snout differ from panda. Finishing marks use two nostrils, not a central circular nose.
- **Bear:** Diagram 1–9. The downward triangle's side wings fold inward on slanting axes and then outward on a second pair of axes, forming pleated ears. Outer tips fold inward at x = ±0.54R and tiny top points fold down, keeping the ears short. After turning, only the front lower layer lifts to form the muzzle; the muzzle tip and remaining bottom corner fold behind to create a flat snout and chin. Multiple ear operations from one diagram panel are separate animation steps. The source's unspecified angles are authored proportions, including the outward ear axes from (±0.176R, 0) to (±0.301R, 0.7R).
- **Koala:** Diagram 1–10. Side corners first meet the bottom point, producing a diamond. Lower flap tips fold outward around axes from (0, 0.25R) to (±0.5R, 0.5R), creating broad projecting ears. The small forehead and slanted ear tips fold in, then the model turns. Front and rear lower tips fold on the same 0.75R line in opposite layer directions to form the pale muzzle and flat chin. The ear tips have separate tags so forehead shaping cannot accidentally shorten them.
- **Tiger:** Diagram 1–11. Starts with the same corner-to-bottom diamond foundation as koala, but outward ear axes are lower: (0, 0.5R) to (±0.5R, 0.7R). These produce short pointed ears. Side margins fold inward at x = ±0.4R; the central forehead folds down while excluding the actual moving ear tips. After turning, separate front/rear chin folds create the pale muzzle. A final small muzzle fold defines the nose. Pen stripes are explicit polygons entirely on the finished head.
- **Mouse:** Diagram 1–6. The downward triangle folds in half with the right half on top. Its outer corner is opened back around a slanted crease from (−0.35R, 0) to (−0.14R, 0.7R). This reflects that corner upward and right to form a large single ear, leaving a long left-pointing muzzle. The lower corner folds behind at y = 0.64R. The side-profile finish has one eye, a triangular left nose and narrow polygon whisker strokes. Selected instead of owl because this verified simple source provides a distinct folded silhouette within the rigid-fold engine.

## Integration and original-session checks

- Export: `faces.mjs` provides `models`, eight entries, category `Djur`.
- Uses engine `select.none` for excluding multiple ear tags; this is the additive selector extension introduced during integration. Its intended predicate is `!selection.none || selection.none.every(tag => !panel.tags.includes(tag))`.
- `face.marks` are explicit `{kind:'ellipse',x,y,rx,ry,fill}` and `{kind:'polygon',points,fill}` shapes so SVG and Blender can show identical pen decoration.
- All eight compiled with the shared engine. Every state and every sampled intermediate fold preserved 22,500 mm² of paper within numerical tolerance; maximum sampled area error was 4.729372449219227e−11 mm². There were no empty crease transitions.
- Final pen marks were checked against the union of the final paper panels. Source photographs/diagrams are research inputs only, never exported assets.
- Blender preview QA was completed for all eight. The first fox/rabbit/pig contact sheet was inspected; corrected panda and the remaining four were rendered with `--preview --samples 8`, two threads, and their final PNGs inspected individually against the diagram sheets. Panda fold direction and bear ear proportions were corrected during this check. All eight were frozen after QA.
- Recovered model IDs and step counts: ravansikte 6; kaninansikte 10; pandaansikte 12; grisansikte 12; bjornansikte 16; koalaansikte 14; tigeransikte 15; musansikte 7.
